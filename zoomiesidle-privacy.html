Privacy Policy — Zoomies Idle

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Zoomies Idle is a cozy idle game. Your village — your rooms, cats, currencies, and progress — is stored only on your device. We do not operate accounts, servers, or analytics.

Advertising

The game shows ads: a small optional rewarded video (the "Tiny TV", which pays coins + treats) and an occasional full-screen ad after minigames. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID to show relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

In-app purchases

Gem packs and the Gold Bank cash-out are sold through Google Play Billing. A one-time "remove ads" purchase is also available. Payments are processed entirely by Google; we never see or store your payment details. Google shares only the fact of a completed purchase with the game so it can grant your gems (or disable ads).

Crash reports

If the game crashes, a report is saved on your device only (Settings → "Last crash report") so you can inspect it. Crash reports are never sent anywhere automatically.

What we collect

Nothing. No name, no email, no location, no contacts, no gameplay analytics. Uninstalling the game deletes everything.

Children

The game is family-friendly and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL and the "Last updated" date revised.
