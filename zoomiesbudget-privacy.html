Privacy Policy — ZoomiesBudget

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Overview

ZoomiesBudget is a personal budget-tracking app. Your financial data stays entirely on your device — we never collect, transmit, or see it. The free version of the app is supported by light advertising; a one-time in-app purchase removes ads permanently.

Your financial data

Everything you enter in the app — transactions, budgets, categories, goals, savings balances, reminders, and settings — is stored exclusively in a database on your own device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of it, and we operate no servers that could receive it. This is unchanged by advertising: your budget data never leaves your device and is never combined with advertising data.

Advertising

The free version of ZoomiesBudget displays ads served by Google AdMob, Google's mobile advertising platform. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID),
  • IP address, device model, operating-system version, and similar technical details,
  • app activity related to the ads shown (for example, whether an ad was viewed or tapped).

This information is collected by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and is used for ad delivery, ad measurement, and fraud prevention. We do not receive any of your financial data through this process.

You can opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device's Settings (Privacy → Ads). Ads will still appear, but they will not be personalized.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details; purchase records are handled by Google under the Google Play terms of service.

Device features

  • Reminders and widgets run entirely on your device using the system notification service.
  • Voice dictation (optional) uses your device's built-in speech input. ZoomiesBudget does not record, store, or transmit audio.
  • Backup and export files you create are saved wherever you choose (for example, your own storage). We never see or handle these files.

Children's privacy

We collect no data from anyone, and no financial data from children. The app targets users 18 and over. Ads served in the app follow Google's policies for age-appropriate advertising.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at burningcolddev@gmail.com.
